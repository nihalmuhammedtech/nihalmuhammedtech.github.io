How I troubleshoot

A repeatable process that moves from symptom to documented fix.

  1. 1

    Define the symptom

    Reproduce the problem and establish exactly who and what is affected.

  2. 2

    Isolate the layer

    Use ping and traceroute to find where connectivity breaks down.

  3. 3

    Capture and analyze

    Apply Wireshark filters to inspect handshakes, retransmissions, and DNS and DHCP exchanges.

  4. 4

    Fix and verify

    Make the change, then confirm that nothing else was disrupted.

  5. 5

    Document

    Record the root cause and the fix in plain language for the next person.
